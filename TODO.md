# Launch checklist

- [ ] Replace sample catalog content, imagery, prices, and demo shipping fees with approved Rijoya data.
- [ ] Set unique `SECRET_KEY`, `ADMIN_EMAIL`, and `ADMIN_PASSWORD`; enable HTTPS and `COOKIE_SECURE=1`.
- [ ] Connect a transactional email provider for password-reset delivery and order notifications.
- [ ] Confirm privacy, returns, delivery, and customer-support policies and publish the appropriate pages.
- [ ] Decide on production database/backups and durable object storage for uploads before scaling beyond one service.
- [ ] Validate the final shipping prices and governorate/city/area coverage with the fulfillment team.
